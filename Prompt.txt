# JES TRANSPORTATION LLC

## PREMIUM MINIMAL TRUCKING WEBSITE — MASTER PROMPT

Build a complete, production-ready website for **JES Transportation LLC**, designed to feel like a premium custom agency project.

The design philosophy is:

**Minimal interface. Rich details. Strong typography. Beautiful photography. Purposeful motion.**

Do not fill the website with bulky icons, excessive cards, gradients, badges, floating elements, or unnecessary animations.

Make the website feel like a sophisticated American transportation brand, not a generic trucking template.

Do not invent cargo specializations, trailer types, routes, service areas, years in business, customer numbers, reviews, safety statistics, insurance limits, certifications, awards, partnerships, or hiring conditions.

The supplied record does not establish detailed service offerings beyond the stated operating authority. Keep service descriptions appropriately general.

## 2. Creative direction

Concept:

**CALIFORNIA TRANSPORTATION × AUTOMOTIVE EDITORIAL × CONTEMPORARY MINIMALISM**

The site should feel confident and understated, using carefully composed truck photography, clean grids, elegant typography, subtle transitions, and generous whitespace.

Avoid:

* Bulky icons everywhere
* Excessive rounded cards
* Glassmorphism and neon gradients
* Generic corporate stock layouts
* Fake statistics and testimonials
* Unnecessary dashboard-style sections
* Decorative animations that slow down navigation

## 3. Visual identity

Use a restrained palette:

* Near black: `#0B0B0B`
* Charcoal: `#181818`
* Warm white: `#F5F3EE`
* Soft gray: `#D8D6D0`
* Muted steel: `#898B8C`
* Restrained burnt orange: `#C75B35`

Use the accent color sparingly for small lines, links, active states, and CTA arrows.

Typography:

* Display: Archivo or Geist
* Body: Inter or Manrope
* Technical information: IBM Plex Mono

Typography and photography should provide the visual character. Do not compensate for a weak layout by adding more UI elements.

## 4. Navigation

Create a thin, minimal navigation bar.

Left:
**JES TRANSPORTATION**

Right:

* Company
* Operations
* Carrier Information
* Contact
* Call ↗

Use text links rather than icon buttons.

On mobile, use a clean full-screen menu with large text and a clearly visible phone number.

## 6. Hero animation

Keep the opening sequence short and elegant.

* Photograph fades in with a subtle scale correction.
* Headline reveals upward through a mask.
* Supporting text fades in afterward.
* CTA arrows move slightly on hover.
* Scroll transition gradually moves the headline out of view.

Avoid character-by-character animation on every heading. Do not delay access to the actual website with a long intro.

## 12. Signature scroll scene

Create one memorable scroll-driven sequence instead of many competing effects.

Use a full-width highway photograph in a pinned section of approximately 180–220vh.

As the visitor scrolls, reveal three words:

**MOVEMENT.**

**DISTANCE.**

**DESTINATION.**

A truck image moves horizontally a short distance while the background shifts subtly at a different speed.

Use GSAP ScrollTrigger if appropriate. Keep the animation smooth, restrained, and fully responsive.

These scenes are illustrative brand storytelling, not representations of actual JES shipments or routes.

On mobile, simplify the effect to short image reveals and fades.

## 19. Minimal interaction system

Use only a few polished interactions:

* Links reveal a thin underline on hover.
* CTA arrows move approximately 4px.
* Images scale slightly on hover.
* Headings reveal through a mask.
* Sections fade or slide into view.
* Navigation gains a subtle divider when scrolling.

Avoid spinning logos, floating icons, animated particles, excessive cursor effects, bouncing cards, and multiple overlapping parallax layers.

A custom cursor is optional, desktop-only, and should be removed if it distracts from navigation.

## 20. Mobile experience

Design mobile intentionally rather than shrinking the desktop layout.

* Large but readable headline
* Full-width photography
* Compact navigation
* Simple vertical section flow
* Shortened animations
* No horizontal overflow
* Readable carrier information
* One-tap calling

A discreet fixed bottom bar may offer **CALL** and **CONTACT**. Do not let it obscure form fields or other content.

## 21. Technical requirements

Preferred stack:

* Next.js
* React
* TypeScript
* Tailwind CSS
* GSAP and ScrollTrigger only where useful

Use semantic HTML, responsive image sizes, AVIF/WebP, lazy loading, optimized fonts, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support.

Aim for excellent Lighthouse performance, accessibility, SEO, and best-practice scores. Avoid adding libraries without a clear reason.

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: disable pinned scenes, smooth scrolling, parallax, and nonessential animation when appropriate.

## 24. Final quality checklist

Before completion, verify:

* The website feels minimal but detailed.
* Photography and typography lead the design.
* There are no unnecessary icon grids or bulky cards.
* The USDOT and MC numbers are accurate.
* The phone number works as a tap-to-call link.
* The carrier information is easy to locate and copy.
* Every company claim is supported by supplied information.
* No safety rating, service area, or performance claim has been invented.
* Scroll effects are smooth and purposeful.
* The site works on mobile and with reduced motion.
* Every form and navigation link functions.
* The site does not look like a generic AI-generated template.

## Final creative direction

Make JES Transportation LLC feel established through **clarity, restraint, composition, typography, photography, and attention to detail**—not through fake scale or decorative UI.

The guiding principle is:

# LESS UI.

# MORE INTENTION.

Every section should have a purpose. Every animation should improve the experience. Every business detail should be accurate.

Deliver a complete, responsive, polished website that is ready for real company content and deployment.
